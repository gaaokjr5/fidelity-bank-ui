Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about banking with Fidelity Bank.

To open a personal account, you will need:

  • Valid means of identification (National ID, Passport, or Driver’s License)
  • Proof of address (Utility bill issued within the last 3 months)
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Completed account opening form

Yes. You can start the process online using our Account Opening Form. In some cases, we may require a brief visit to your nearest branch for verification.

Minimum opening balances vary by account type:

  • Personal Savings — NGN 0 to NGN 5,000
  • Current Account — NGN 10,000
  • Premium Savings — NGN 50,000

Contact your nearest branch for details on specific products.

Daily transaction limits depend on your channel and account tier:

  • Mobile App — Up to NGN 5,000,000 daily
  • Internet Banking — Up to NGN 10,000,000 daily
  • USSD Code — Up to NGN 300,000 daily
  • ATM — NGN 500,000 daily withdrawal limit

Limits can be increased upon request at any branch.

If a transaction fails or your account is debited without success, please:

  • Wait 24 hours — most transactions auto-reverse
  • Keep your transaction reference number
  • Submit details via our Complaint & Feedback Form
  • Or call our contact center for immediate assistance

Transfers to other Nigerian banks typically complete within minutes. During peak periods or system maintenance, it may take up to 24 hours. If funds do not arrive within this window, please contact us.
