Security Awareness

Protecting your finances and personal information is our shared responsibility. Learn how to stay safe online and offline.

Official Notice

Fidelity Bank will NEVER ask for your PIN, password, token code, or full card details via phone, email, SMS, or social media.

What We Will NEVER Ask

If anyone claiming to be from Fidelity Bank contacts you requesting any of the following, it is a scam — do not share the information.

Your PIN or Password

We never ask for your login credentials or personal identification numbers.

Full Card Details

Never share your full card number, CVV, or expiry code with anyone.

Token or OTP Codes

One-time passwords are for YOUR use only — we do not need them to process transactions.

Account Verification via Link

Do not click links asking you to "re-verify" or "upgrade" your account — always type our address directly.

Common Scams to Watch For

SMS & WhatsApp Impersonation

Messages claiming your account will be suspended or blocked unless you click a link and provide details.

What to do: Delete the message and report it. Do not click any links.

Fake Job Offers

Emails or social media posts offering employment at Fidelity Bank requiring payment for "forms" or "screening."

What to do: All our recruitment is listed on our official Careers page only.

Unexpected "Prize" or "Loan"

Notifications that you have won money or been approved for a loan — but you must pay a fee first to release funds.

What to do: If you did not enter, you did not win. Legitimate loans do not require upfront fees.

Phishing Emails

Messages that look official but use slightly different spellings in the sender address or ask you to log in through a non-official site.

What to do: Check the sender address carefully. Our official domain ends in @fidelitybankplc.com.

Your Security Best Practices

Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords combining letters, numbers, and symbols. Change them regularly.

Verify Before You Act

Always check the website address. Look for the padlock icon and confirm it says fidelitybankplc.com.

Log Out Always

End your session properly when using shared or public devices.

Monitor Activity

Check your statements regularly. Report any unfamiliar transactions immediately.

Report Suspicious Activity

If you suspect you have received a fraudulent message or shared your details, contact us immediately through our official channels.
