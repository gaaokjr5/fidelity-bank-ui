Cookie Policy

This policy explains how Fidelity Bank Nigeria uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, analyze website traffic, and personalize content.

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site. Cookies help the website remember your preferences, improve your experience, and make interactions faster and more secure.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies on our website:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary to provide you with services available through our website and to use some of its features. They enable core functionality such as page navigation and access to secure areas. Without these cookies, the website cannot function properly.

Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. They help us improve how our website works, for example by ensuring that users can easily find what they are looking for.

Functionality Cookies

These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make — such as your preferred language, region, and customized settings. The purpose of these cookies is to provide you with a more personal experience and to avoid you having to re-enter your preferences every time you visit our site.

Strictly Necessary Security Cookies

Used to authenticate users, prevent fraudulent use of login credentials, and protect customer data from unauthorized access. These security cookies are required for banking services and cannot be disabled.

3. Specific Cookies We Use

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser — essential for maintaining your session while logged in.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings so they remain consistent across future visits.
  • Security Cookies: Help verify your identity and protect your account during your session.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us measure usage and performance so we can improve our website.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. We may use services such as website analytics tools to understand usage patterns — these services may set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies. We ensure any third-party services used comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and applicable banking regulations.

5. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish by using the settings available on your browser. You can delete all cookies that are already on your device, and you can set most browsers to prevent them from being placed. Detailed instructions can be found in your browser's help section.

Please Note: If you disable cookies — especially Essential and Security Cookies — some parts of our website may not function properly, and secure banking services may become unavailable.

6. How Often Will We Update This Policy?

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or services. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

7. Where to Find Further Information

For more information about how we protect your personal data, please review our Privacy Policy, which explains our data protection practices in detail.
